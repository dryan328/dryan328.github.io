Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Chromaspekt (“the App”) is a Mac desktop application designed for local LUT previewing and color evaluation.

Information We Collect

Chromaspekt does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information.

The App works entirely on your Mac.

  • It does not require an account or login.
  • It does not connect to any external servers for core functionality.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or crash reporting services that collect personal data.
  • It only accesses files that you explicitly open or save.

Local Files

Chromaspekt only accesses files that you explicitly open or save (such as videos, images, LUT files, Looks, and Projects). These files remain on your device and are never uploaded or shared by the App.
