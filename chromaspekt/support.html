Help and answers for Chromaspekt on Mac

Chromaspekt is a Mac desktop application designed for local LUT previewing and color evaluation. If you need help, have a question, or want to report a problem, you’re in the right place.

Contact Support

The fastest way to reach us is by email at . We aim to reply within a few business days.

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Your Chromaspekt version (found in the Chromaspekt menu → About Chromaspekt).
  • Your macOS version and Mac model (Apple menu → About This Mac).
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps to reproduce the problem, if you know them.
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if they help illustrate the issue.
  • Details about the media or LUT file involved (for example, file type and where it came from). Please don’t send files containing confidential material unless we ask.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Chromaspekt require an account or an internet connection?

No. Chromaspekt works entirely on your Mac and does not require an account, login, or connection to external servers for its core functionality.

Where are my files stored?

Your videos, images, LUT files, Looks, and Projects stay on your device. Chromaspekt only accesses files that you explicitly open or save, and never uploads or shares them.

A file won’t open, or doesn’t look right. What should I try?

  • Confirm the file isn’t corrupted by opening it in another app, such as QuickTime Player or Preview.
  • Check that the file is stored locally or fully downloaded, not still syncing from a cloud service.
  • Make sure you’re running the latest version of Chromaspekt from the Mac App Store.
  • Restart Chromaspekt and try again.

My LUT doesn’t load or the result looks unexpected.

Check that the LUT file is complete and not damaged, and try loading it again. LUTs are designed for specific input color spaces, so a LUT applied to footage in a different color space can produce unexpected results. If the problem continues, email us the LUT’s file type and a description of what you’re seeing.

The app is running slowly or behaving unexpectedly.

  • Quit and reopen Chromaspekt.
  • Close other resource-intensive apps to free up memory and GPU capacity.
  • Restart your Mac, and make sure macOS is up to date.
  • Large or high-resolution media can be demanding. Let us know your Mac model and the type of media you’re using if performance remains an issue.

How do I update Chromaspekt?

Updates are delivered through the Mac App Store. Open the App Store, choose Updates in the sidebar, and install any available update for Chromaspekt.

I purchased Chromaspekt but can’t access it on another Mac.

Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase. Purchases, refunds, and billing are handled by Apple. For those issues, please visit Apple Support.
